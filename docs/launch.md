## 1. Hacker News — "Show HN"

**Title:** Show HN: Tailor AI – self-hosted AI that scores and tailors your CV to any job, 100% local

**Body (comment under the post):**

> I built Tailor AI because every "ATS checker" I tried uploaded my CV to someone's cloud. For a job search that's the most sensitive document you own — so this runs entirely on your machine. Your CV, your job data, even your LLM API key never leave your computer.
>
> What it does:
> - Scrapes 17+ job sources (LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, Naukri + free built-ins: Arbeitnow, RemoteOK, Dice, Reed, MyCareersFuture, Gupy, JobsCh…)
> - AI-scores any job against your CV: match %, skill gaps, missing keywords
> - Generates an ATS-optimized tailored CV as PDF for any job description
> - Real-time LinkedIn recruiter posts (free engine, no token)
> - Recruiter CRM + AI-drafted cold emails via your own SMTP
> - Mock AI interviews grounded in the actual job description
>
> One command install, runs in Docker: `curl -fsSL https://github.com/Atanub707/Tailor-AI/raw/main/scripts/install.sh | bash`
>
> MIT licensed, one-click auto-updates built in. Would love feedback — especially on the ATS scoring approach and the free LinkedIn-post engine (which is the part I'm most proud of: pure fetch + parsing, zero paid tokens).
